# Public foundation surface

## Scope and mode

Homepage persuades an invited employee or manager that Cloxa is deliberately small and traceable. Authentication and workspace routes operate as honest shells: they explain what follows without presenting inactive controls as working product.

## Audience and action

Audience is one Flemish organization with one worksite and 5–20 employees. Primary action is “Naar aanmelden.” Secondary action explains registration through a personal invitation.

## Content proof

Show future chain as four neutral steps: invite and sign in, record hours, review correction, export approved data. State foundation boundary, single-organization scope, no payroll or overtime calculation, and no public registration. Use future tense; make no production, compliance, or workflow-completion claims.

## Visual direction

Quality-control traveler docket in daylight paper, deep ink, cobalt fields, amber marks, ruled sections, and restrained depth. Desktop first viewport is one split dossier; mobile becomes one vertical trace. Keep exact 320px support, self-hosted condensed display type, solid accessible focus, and one dominant sign-in action.

## Constraints

Dutch (`nl-BE`) copy stays centralized. No database schema, active authentication form, time controls, approval controls, exports, history, offline behavior, or service worker in this foundation.

## Unresolved

Formal brand identity, production evidence, and role-aware post-login routing wait for later work. Next foundation task is Supabase schema and RLS policies.
